6. Information Requests and File Inspection / Copy

The healthcare provider provides information about the minor at the request of the parent(s)/guardian. In doing so, he takes into account the limitations in connection with good caremanship.

Upon request by one parent, the healthcare provider also provides the written or digital information given to this parent to the other parent with authority.

7. Exercise of Data Rights Regarding the File

If a parent or guardian exercises their right to a copy of or inspection of the file or another right concerning the minor's file, the healthcare provider responds within one month of the parent(s)/guardian's request. If the healthcare provider needs more time to comply with the request, he informs the parent(s)/guardian within one month, stating the timeframe within which the parent(s)/guardian can expect a response. He adheres to the maximum extension period of two months.

With the exception of the right to inspect and receive a copy of the file, parents with authority can only exercise rights regarding the minor's file jointly.

In evaluating a request to exercise a right, the healthcare provider considers the limitations due to good caremanship and the protection of third-party privacy.

8. Provision of Information to Parent Without Authority

The healthcare provider provides general and factual information about the treatment of the minor at the request of a parent without authority, unless:

  • He is not permitted to provide the information to the parent with authority, or
  • The best interests of the child oppose the provision of the information.

The healthcare provider discusses the request with the parent possessing authority. Prior to this, he informs the parent without authority.

9. Treatment File

9.1 File Content

  • Session Reports: The healthcare provider makes a report of every treatment session and keeps it in the file. The records show which examinations were performed, their outcomes, which treatment was given, and its effects.
  • Notes: The healthcare provider notes information provided to and received from the parent(s)/guardian and minor, treatment consent, treatment evaluation, further agreements/modifications, third-party disclosure consent, and relevant telephone conversations.
  • Correspondence: The healthcare provider retains correspondence with the parent(s)/guardian, minor, other healthcare providers, or health insurers.
  • Other Information: All other information relevant to treatment or accountability is retained.

9.2 File Retention

The healthcare provider retains the file for 20 years from the date of the last modification in the file or 20 years after the minor reaches 18 years of age, whichever date comes later, unless longer retention is required due to good caremanship.

10. Confidentiality and Data Handling

10.1 Confidentiality

The healthcare provider keeps confidential all information about the minor and their parent(s)/guardian obtained in their professional capacity. Information is provided to third parties (other than the minor) only with legal or judicial grounds, including:

  • Explicit consent from the parent(s)/guardian;
  • A legal obligation (if statutory conditions are met);
  • A statutory reporting right (if statutory conditions are met).

Exceptions to confidentiality obligations include:

  1. Provision of information to the parent(s)/guardian of the minor;
  2. Providing access to substitutes or replacements of the healthcare provider as necessary for care;
  3. Providing access to persons directly involved in executing the treatment agreement as necessary for their work.

10.2 Exchange of Information with GP & Healthcare Providers

Information about the minor is shared orally or in writing with the GP or other healthcare providers outside the practice exclusively with the consent of the parent(s)/guardian (excluding replacements/substitutes). Providing information about the parent(s)/guardian requires their direct consent.

10.3 GDPR Compliance

The healthcare provider complies with obligations under the GDPR (AVG) to protect data. The privacy statement on the practice website details data handling procedures and rights under the GDPR.
