# Compliance (blocking)

Source: NVKH "Wat mag op de website" (2022) and RBCZ "Beoordelingscriteria websitevermelding". Everything on the site counts as advertising. A violation here is **Blocker** severity.

## Prohibited content
- [ ] No homeopathic remedy names anywhere in visible text, alt text, meta description, JSON-LD, or Open Graph (Arnica, Pulsatilla, Nux vomica, Belladonna, Sulphur, etc.).
- [ ] No "genezen", "genees", "cure", "cured", "heal(s) you", "remedy for <disease>" or any promise of results. "Not scientifically proven" statements stay on the site.
- [ ] No self-written patient cases or case studies. Testimonials only if clearly written by the patient, with consent.
- [ ] No disease-specific treatment claims that imply efficacy ("treats cancer", "stops asthma").
- [ ] No unverifiable comparative claims ("only", "best", "no one else", "unique in the Netherlands").
- [ ] RBCZ/NVKH logos used only for the individual therapist, never as an endorsement of the website or of unrelated services.

## Information that must remain
- [ ] Practice address for each location (Uithoorn: Antoon Coolenlaan 41, 1422 GN; Aalsmeer: Stationsweg 38, 1431 EG).
- [ ] Reachability: phone and email.
- [ ] How to get there: car, public transport, parking, and **wheelchair yes/no** (Uithoorn: no, 2nd floor via stairs, online is the alternative; Aalsmeer: yes).
- [ ] Name plus KvK 85796700.
- [ ] Education and experience, consistent with CLAUDE.md facts (BHMS, North Gujarat University, Nuffic-assessed, 25 years).
- [ ] Rates including outside office hours (€95). Rates match across home, FAQ, terms, location pages, EN and NL.
- [ ] "Not reimbursed from basic insurance, possibly from supplementary: check your policy."
- [ ] Clear information on services and how a consultation runs, including the "bring a medication list" note.
- [ ] Quality statement: five-yearly review under RBCZ/NVKH criteria; TCZ disciplinary law.
- [ ] Privacy statement: purpose, 20-year retention, security, rights, AVG/GDPR, professional secrecy, and Web3Forms named as a processor.
- [ ] Complaints procedure naming: Quasir Expertisecentrum klachtbehandeling (free, 6 weeks), Stichting Zorggeschil (binding ruling within 6 months), TCZ (tcz.nu).
- [ ] HTTPS for any form; no cookies set (site uses none).

## Cancellation fees
- [ ] A website notice alone is not enough. The fee must be stated in the booking confirmation email or invoice before the first consultation. Site text must say this is confirmed by email.
- [ ] Cancellation window stated consistently as 24 hours.

## Facts that must not drift
Any change to a number, address, registration number, price, or hour must be checked against the "Practice facts" and "Purpose and positioning" sections of `CLAUDE.md` in all places it appears (EN, NL, JSON-LD, footer, location pages, terms).

## Invoices (not on the site)
Flag any change that adds invoice content to the site. If invoice templates are touched, check against rule 8 in CLAUDE.md; never include the BSN.
