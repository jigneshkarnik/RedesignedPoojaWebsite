What to expect

The first consultation takes about 90 minutes. We go through your complaints, sleep, digestion, energy, emotions, stress and family history, at your own pace. Afterwards I study your case and choose a remedy, with clear instructions on how to take it. Follow-up consultations take 30 to 60 minutes. Please bring a list of any medication you take.

Homeopathy is a complementary approach. It is not scientifically proven to be effective beyond placebo and does not replace care from your GP or specialist. Please never stop or change prescribed medication without speaking to your doctor.
