Community work

Giving back to the community I live in is an important part of who I am.

Working with local organisations

I volunteer with Stichting Sanskriti Aur Sanskar and Uithoorn voor Elkaar, contributing to health-awareness events and outreach, and bridging Indian and Dutch culture.

Weekly volunteering at Het Hoge Heem

Each week I spend time with residents of this elderly care home in Uithoorn, helping them enjoy meaningful, fulfilling days. These visits mean a great deal to me.

India Day, Uithoorn

On 23 November 2024 Stichting Sanskriti Aur Sanskar and Amstelring Het Hoge Heem celebrated India Day together, with people, traditions and food coming together in harmony.

Women's health talk

Together with Uithoorn voor Elkaar and Stichting Sanskriti Aur Sanskar I gave an interactive talk at the Vrouwenmiddag about hormonal balance in everyday life.
